# ZYS Recording — Opportunity Radar

A lightweight, source-verified opportunity tracker for international motorsport and car-culture photography.

## Goal
Identify actionable opportunities for ZYS Recording, prioritizing:
- Drift and motorsport media accreditation (Europe and UAE)
- Paid photography commissions with racing teams, event organizers and automotive brands
- Editorial submissions to car-culture and motorsport publications
- Cultural grants and documentary photography open calls

## Quality standard
Every opportunity must have:
- Organization and opportunity name
- Direct official source URL
- Type (accreditation / paid commission / editorial / grant)
- Country and event date if applicable
- Deadline (or "not published")
- Eligibility and required portfolio
- Contact or official application route
- Date verified and status (open / upcoming / unverified / closed)
- Fit explanation for ZYS Recording

**Never label a lead 'open' without a verified current official source. Never invent contact details or deadlines.**

## Workflow
1. Collect leads from official organizer/publication/grant pages.
2. Deduplicate by organization + opportunity + year.
3. Verify dates and eligibility.
4. Score fit based on portfolio, realistic access, cost, and career impact.
5. Review top opportunities weekly.
6. Draft tailored applications only after human approval.
7. Track submission dates and replies.

## Privacy
Do not commit unpublished photographs, private email addresses, credentials, API keys, contact lists or client information to this public repository. Use a private repository for sensitive tracking data.
